Premier League Predictor

How this works: every team gets an Elo rating that shifts after each result, plus an attack/defense strength estimated from actual goals scored and conceded. Those feed a Poisson model that turns two teams' strengths into a full probability for every possible scoreline -- not just win/draw/loss. The season is then simulated 10,000 times using those probabilities to get title, top-4, and relegation odds.

Everything updates weekly as real results come in, and manually-logged injuries/suspensions (see Team News below) temporarily adjust a team's strength for exactly the fixtures affected, then expire automatically. A team playing again within 4 days of a cup or European fixture also gets a small short-rest penalty, based on a manually-logged fixture date, not a full match result -- so this works even for European away trips against clubs we have no data on at all. Backtested against real historical seasons -- see the project README for how well it holds up.
